# Code of conduct

Atrinik is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participate in good faith, assume honest intent, give actionable feedback, and
respect different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and skill levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, sexualized attention, publishing another
person's private information, or retaliation against someone who reports a
concern.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict
participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, private where possible, and focused on protecting affected
people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Atrinik organization owners through
[GitHub's organization contact page](https://github.com/orgs/atrinik/people).
Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report.
